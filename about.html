About Speak Empowered

Great ideas deserve to be heard.

We help credible voices become more visible to the people their message can serve.

Why Speak Empowered Exists

Expertise alone doesn't create visibility.

Great leaders, business owners, authors, and experts often have something meaningful to say but lack the time, relationships, or strategy needed to consistently get their message in front of new audiences.

Speak Empowered exists to help close that gap. Through targeted podcast outreach, thoughtful guest positioning, press visibility, and ongoing marketing support, we help voices worth hearing reach more of the people who need to hear them.

Built From Experience, Not Theory

We've experienced what intentional media visibility can create.

Speak Empowered was founded by Nathan R Mitchell, Founder of Lead Empowered LLC, Forbes Coaches Council Contributor, and Host of Lead Empowered TV.

Since launching Lead Empowered TV in December 2025, Nathan has experienced firsthand what can happen when a meaningful message is paired with intentional media strategy.

In less than a year, Lead Empowered TV has grown to more than 28,700 YouTube subscribers and approximately 6,000 monthly viewers on e360TV.

That experience helped inspire Speak Empowered: a representation company designed to help other credible voices gain access to conversations, audiences, and opportunities that can expand their influence.
