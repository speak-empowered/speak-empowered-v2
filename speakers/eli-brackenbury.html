About Eli

A grounded voice for people searching, rebuilding, and rediscovering faith.

Eli Brackenbury is a writer, father, and EHS professional whose unexpected spiritual awakening at fifty reshaped every part of his life. After years marked by divorce, grief, striving, and quiet spiritual dryness, an ordinary encounter with God sparked a new journey of healing, purpose, and renewed faith.

His message resonates with skeptics, seekers, wounded Christians, and people returning to faith after years away. Rather than preaching, Eli brings a real-life, non-churchy approach to spiritual growth through honest stories about identity, mental health, relationships, leadership, and everyday spirituality.

His debut book, Finding God at 50: A Mid-Life Awakening to Grace, Purpose and Presence, is the first in a planned series exploring spiritual transformation across the seasons of life.

Suggested Interview Questions

  1. What is the difference between religion and relationship?
  2. What would you tell someone who wants to get closer to God but does not know where to begin?
  3. How do you speak to skeptics, wounded Christians, and seekers without sounding preachy?
  4. Why do so many people hit a spiritual wall in their 40s and 50s?
  5. You have said finding God at 50 felt like something that happened to you more than something you chose. What do you mean by that?
  6. What does a restored man look like, and how is he different from a man who has simply been successful?
  7. Where does culture's definition of a strong man break down, and what does Scripture offer instead?
