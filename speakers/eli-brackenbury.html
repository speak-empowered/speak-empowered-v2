About Eli

A relatable voice for people searching, rebuilding, and beginning again.

Eli Brackenbury is a writer, father, and environmental health and safety professional whose unexpected spiritual awakening at fifty reshaped the way he viewed God, purpose, and himself.

After seasons marked by divorce, grief, striving, and spiritual dryness, Eli found renewed faith in the ordinary moments of life. His message is especially meaningful for skeptics, seekers, people returning to faith, and anyone wondering whether it is too late to start again.

His debut memoir, Finding God at 50: A Mid-Life Awakening to Grace, Purpose and Presence, was named a 2026 International Book Awards finalist in the Spirituality: Inspirational category.
